The grouping is saved without dropping the configured music folders
===================================================================

Driven through the running application: every write below was made by clicking
a control in the window, not by editing the file.

Starting config, with music folders configured as a user would have them:

  {
    "group_mode": "artist",
    "view_mode": "grid",
    "music_roots": [
      "/tmp/shuffle-demo/home/Music"
    ]
  }

After clicking "Album", then the list-view button, in the running window:

  {
    "group_mode": "album",
    "view_mode": "list",
    "music_roots": [
      "/tmp/shuffle-demo/home/Music"
    ]
  }

Both settings the click owns are updated and music_roots survives, which is
what the read-modify-write in config.py exists for: before it, each writer
dumped only what it knew about.

Restart check: with "artist" saved, the application was closed with its own
close button and launched again. It came back grouped by artist with the
Artist toggle lit - 04-grouping-restored-after-restart-2x.png.
