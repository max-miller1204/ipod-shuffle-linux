Grid rebuilds during one startup scan of a 600-track library
============================================================

The real application, launched against a library of 600 tagged MP3s in 120
albums, with a counter wrapped around IpodWindow._refresh_current_view - the
call that clears the album grid and rebuilds every card in it. Nothing else
was stubbed: same interpreter, same display, same music folders on disk.

Rebuilding the grid destroys the widget under the pointer, which is what the
user saw as text flashing while hovering.

  $ /usr/bin/python3.12 /tmp/count-repaints.py /tmp/base-repo 25 "before"
  before (base 598f93c): 48 scan batches published, 49 full grid rebuilds, 600 tracks in the library
  before (base 598f93c): rebuilds ran from 3.48s to 18.95s
  before (base 598f93c): shortest gap between two rebuilds 23ms
  before (base 598f93c): batches ran from 3.48s to 18.42s

  $ /usr/bin/python3.12 /tmp/count-repaints.py . 25 "after"
  after  (this branch): 48 scan batches published, 1 full grid rebuilds, 600 tracks in the library
  after  (this branch): rebuilds ran from 3.71s to 3.71s
  after  (this branch): batches ran from 3.52s to 3.71s

The same 48 batches arrive either way. Before, each one rebuilt the grid, and
the rebuilds were still going 15 seconds after the scan started - the window
was unusable under the pointer for that whole time, with rebuilds as little as
23ms apart. After, the batches are absorbed into a single rebuild and the grid
settles 0.2s after the first batch lands.
