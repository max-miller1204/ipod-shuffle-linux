Files on disk after the run captured in the screenshots beside this file.

The GUI ran against a disposable HOME (/tmp/pl-e2e-home) and a stand-in iPod
volume, so nothing here touched the real device mounted on the machine. Only
iPod detection and HOME were replaced; the window, the playlist store and
ipod-sync.sh are the shipped code.

$ ls ~/Music/Playlists
Gym.m3u
Road Trip.m3u

$ cat ~/Music/Playlists/Road Trip.m3u
#EXTM3U
/tmp/pl-e2e-home/Music/Ana Petrov/Field Notes/01 - Paper Boats.mp3
/tmp/pl-e2e-home/Music/Ana Petrov/Field Notes/02 - Coastal Road.mp3
/tmp/pl-e2e-home/Music/youtube/Kevin MacLeod/Cipher [N0KuBFK9r24].mp3

  Paper Boats came from the search results, Coastal Road from the library
  table, and Cipher from a YouTube result the app downloaded first. Paper
  Boats sits first because it was dragged there from third place; the file
  was rewritten in the order the window shows.

$ cat ~/Music/Playlists/Gym.m3u
#EXTM3U
/tmp/pl-e2e-home/Music/youtube/Kevin MacLeod/Cipher [N0KuBFK9r24].mp3

  Gym was made from the ⋯ menu while moving a track into it, then emptied
  again, then given the same YouTube video a second time. The second add
  downloaded nothing - the video was already in the music folder - and the
  track was still filed into the playlist, found by its video id.

$ ls '/tmp/pl-e2e-home/iPod'          # the stand-in volume, after Sync
iPod_Control
Made Elsewhere.m3u
Road Trip.m3u

$ cat '/tmp/pl-e2e-home/iPod/Road Trip.m3u'
#EXTM3U
iPod_Control/Music/Field Notes/01 - Paper Boats.mp3
iPod_Control/Music/Field Notes/02 - Coastal Road.mp3

  Written by ipod-sync.sh from the playlist file above, in the same order,
  with the entries rewritten relative to the volume root. Gym.m3u was on the
  device after the first sync and is gone after the second, because its last
  track had been taken out - the song itself stayed on the iPod.

$ cat /tmp/pl-e2e-home/iPod/iPod_Control/.sync-options
--playlist-voiceover

  Switched on by the sync itself: a playlist reaching a screenless device has
  no identity without a spoken name.
