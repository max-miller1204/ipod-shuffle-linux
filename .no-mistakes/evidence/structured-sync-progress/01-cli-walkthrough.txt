The scripts, driven from a shell, reporting themselves twice: once to the
terminal for a person and once as JSON on a descriptor for the GUI's sync
bar. Everything below ran in one temporary directory holding a synthetic
iPod and a source album whose names are the awkward ones.

$ ls -1 "Odd Album"
01 - Plain.mp3
02 - Say "hi", it's \ fine.mp3
03 - Line
break.mp3
04 - Dangling.mp3
cover.flac
[exit 0]

(the third name holds a real newline, which is why it reads as two lines)

=== 6. A reader that walks away mid-copy ================================
Closing the window during a sync is this: the reader takes one line and
goes. A shell writing to a pipe nobody holds is killed outright, which
would have left the iPod half written.

$ ipod-sync.sh --ipod ./iPod-spare --progress-json "Odd Album" \
      3>&1 1> copy.txt | head -1
{"event": "start", "schema": 1, "script": "sync"}
[exit 0]
